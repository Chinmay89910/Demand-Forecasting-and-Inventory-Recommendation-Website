The 5-Step Daily Operating Flow

Here is how RestockIQ optimizes food retailer operations every single day

1
6:00 AM • Morning Prep

AI Demand Forecast & Newsvendor Buffer

🌅

Before morning baking begins, the Autoregressive Gradient-Boosted Trees (GBDT) model predicts today's demand based on day-of-week, weather conditions, and sales velocity.

Optimal Order Target (Q*) Q* = Expected Demand + (z × σ)

Using Newsvendor Critical Ratio ($CR = 0.60 \implies z = +0.2533$), the system calculates the ideal safety stock buffer so you never over-bake or run out during the 8:00 AM rush.

2
10:00 AM • Shelf Tracking

Your Products & Perishable Classification

🥐

Add your shop's items into the Products catalog. Define selling price, unit cost, and tag whether items are perishable (like fresh croissants) or non-perishable (like packaged coffee beans).

Perishability Safeguard 18–24 Hour Shelf Life

Perishable items cannot be sold the next morning. RestockIQ prioritizes these items in restock calculations to prevent full inventory write-offs.

3
2:00 PM • Afternoon Action

Real-Time Waste Alerts & 20% Markdown

⚡

At mid-afternoon, if customer sales velocity is slower than expected (< 60% pace ratio), an urgent Waste Alert fires directly on your dashboard.

One-Click Mitigations Revenue Recovery

With one click, activate a 20% Promotional Discount at your POS to clear surplus pastries before closing, or flag remaining units for local charity food donation.

4
6:00 PM • Store Closing

Evening Check-In & Perishable Zero-Reset

🌙

Closing staff walks through the fast touch-stepper audit to count remaining inventory on the shelves before locking up for the night.

Perishable Zero-Reset Clean Slate Every Day

One click zeroes out all unsold fresh baked goods so tomorrow morning's forecast always starts fresh without ghost stock carrying over.

5
Ongoing Operations • Continuous Learning

Sales CSV Ingestion & Profit Simulation Reports

📈

Whenever you have historical sales files (from Square, Clover, Shopify POS, or manual logs), upload them in Import CSV. The system automatically validates formats, dates, and non-negative numbers to retrain your shop's predictive model.

Financial Benchmark Reports Compare your actual reduced waste ($ saved) and profit margins directly against traditional "gut feel" static ordering.
